# Career Horoscope for Girls in Tech 🔮✨

A playful, sarcastic career prediction app designed to make tech girls smile while navigating the wild world of tech careers. Get your personalized horoscope based on your tech stack, sleep schedule, and caffeine dependency!

## Features

- **Career Luck 🌟** - Hilarious predictions about your tech career with varying degrees of reality-checking (high luck, mid vibes, or chaotic energy)
- **Burnout Warning 🚨** - Sarcastic but caring warnings about your wellbeing based on sleep and caffeine levels, from "legend behavior" to "call emergency services"
- **Internship Prophecy 💼** - Brutally honest predictions about your job search journey (because we've all been there)
- **Daily Affirmation 💕** - Encouraging reminders that imposter syndrome is lying to you
- **Delulu Mode ✨** - Toggle for extra dramatic, completely unrealistic predictions (manifesting energy at its peak)
- **Christmas Vibes 🎄** - Animated falling snowflakes and a cozy pastel aesthetic with festive holiday energy

## How It Works

1. Select your tech stack vibe (Frontend, Backend, Data/AI, Mobile, Full Stack, or "I'm confused 😭")
2. Choose your sleep schedule (be honest bestie)
3. Rate your caffeine dependency level
4. Toggle "Delulu Mode" for extra chaos (optional)
5. Click **✨ Get My Career Horoscope ✨** to reveal your destiny
6. Get another reading whenever you need more motivation/chaos

## Tech Stack

- **React 18** with TypeScript for a solid, type-safe foundation
- **Tailwind CSS** for beautiful, responsive styling
- **Lucide React** for clean, modern icons
- **Vite** for lightning-fast development and builds

## Design Highlights

- Gradient backgrounds (pink, purple, blue) with a cozy holiday theme
- Smooth animations: slide-up effects, pulsing sparkles, bouncing briefcases
- Snowflake background animation that loops continuously
- Interactive hover states on result cards
- Fully responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Accessibility-friendly with proper contrast ratios

## Getting Started

# Build for production
